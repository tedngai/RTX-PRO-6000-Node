# Candidate: mem-fraction 0.90 + CUDA-graph trims

Date: 2026-10-16 ~04:17–04:21 UTC. Launcher:
`configs/docker/candidates/qwen27b-mf90-graphtrim.sh`. One launch, no retry.
Not promoted.

## What changed vs the validated c12 config

- `--mem-fraction-static` 0.85 → 0.90
- Decode CUDA graphs trimmed to `[1, 2, 4, 8, 12]` (was an implicit list
  through 12, itself already cut from the default 256 by the admission cap)
- Prefill CUDA graphs trimmed to 10 shapes `[4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256,
  512, 1024, 2048]` (was 42 shapes). Confirmed in `/server_info` and in
  the capture log.

Admission, DFLASH D=4, and mamba ratio 2.0 / `extra_buffer` unchanged.
